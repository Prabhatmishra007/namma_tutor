Questions from BTM Layout parents

Before you book.

Do you have tutors available in BTM Layout right now?

Almost always, yes. Send the class, board and subjects and we will come back within 24 hours with two or three matching profiles, their experience and their hourly rate. If a specialist subject is scarce that week, we will tell you straight away rather than keep you waiting.

How much does home tuition cost in BTM Layout?

₹300–₹500 an hour for Class 1–5, ₹400–₹600 for Class 6–8, ₹500–₹700 for Class 9–10 and ₹550–₹800 for Class 11–12. You are told the exact figure before the free demo, never after.

Can we start with online classes instead?

Yes. Many BTM Layout families start online for senior subjects, where getting the right specialist matters more than the tutor's postcode, then switch to home classes if they prefer.

Are the tutors verified?

Every tutor is interviewed by us, and government ID plus qualification documents are checked before they are assigned a student. For home tuition you also get the tutor's name and photo before the first visit.

What if the tutor is not the right fit?

Tell us after the demo and we arrange another match at no cost. The same applies later — a bad fit helps nobody.
