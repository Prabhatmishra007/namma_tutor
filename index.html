Questions parents ask

Before you book a demo.

Do you charge a registration fee?

Yes — a one-time ₹99. Most agencies charge ₹590 or more just to show you a tutor profile; we're not that guy. The ₹99 covers tutor matching and your free demo class, and that is the only fee we ever take: no agency commission, no joining fee, no lock-in package. Everything after that goes straight to your tutor, per class.

How much does home tuition cost in Bangalore?

₹300–₹500 an hour for Class 1–5, ₹400–₹600 for Class 6–8, ₹500–₹700 for Class 9–10 and ₹550–₹800 for Class 11–12. The exact rate depends on how far the tutor travels and how experienced they are. You are told the number before the demo class, not after.

What if we don't like the tutor?

Tell us after the demo and we arrange a different match at no cost. If it goes wrong later, the same applies — a bad fit helps nobody.

Can students outside India take classes?

Yes. Online one-to-one classes run worldwide over video call, scheduled in your local time. Families in the UAE, Singapore, the UK, the US, Australia and Canada study with us this way.

Are the tutors verified?

Every tutor is interviewed by us, and government ID plus qualification documents are checked before they are assigned a student. For home tuition, you also get the tutor's name and photo before the first visit.

Do you teach IB and IGCSE?

Yes — alongside CBSE, ICSE, Karnataka State Board and NIOS. IB and IGCSE tutors are fewer, so allow us an extra day or two to shortlist.

How do I buy the notes and guess papers?

Open a sample on the study material page, then message us on WhatsApp with the class and chapter. We send the price and a payment link, and the files reach you the same day.
