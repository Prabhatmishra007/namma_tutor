Good to know

How the material is put together.

Which boards is the material written for?

CBSE first, then ICSE. State Board and IGCSE sets exist for selected chapters — ask before ordering so we can tell you what is ready.

Are the answers checked?

Yes. Every numerical answer is worked twice and cross-checked against the board's marking scheme before a file is released.

How do I receive the files?

On WhatsApp or email, usually the same day. PDFs download and print; interactive notes are a single HTML file that opens in any browser and keeps working without internet.

Can you build material for a chapter that isn't ready yet?

Often, yes. Send the chapter name and the board, and we will tell you the turnaround and the price before you commit to anything.

Do tuition students pay extra for this?

No. If your child is taking classes with us, the notes for the chapters being taught come with the class.
